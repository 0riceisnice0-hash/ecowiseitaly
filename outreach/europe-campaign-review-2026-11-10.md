## Contact rules and launch gate

- Switzerland: the [Swiss data-protection authority](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en/advertising-marketing) describes opt-in as the general rule for email advertising and a limited existing-customer exception. The [communications regulator](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/when-is-mass-sending-allowed) also states that automated mass advertising requires explicit prior consent. Do not treat public school inboxes, individually addressed messages or the Beau Soleil relationship as permission to market to unrelated Swiss schools. Use existing relationships/referrals or an appropriately checked contact route; a mass email asking for consent is not a workaround.
- France: [CNIL's professional-email guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-prospection-commerciale-par-courrier-electronique-sms-mms-et-automate-dappel) distinguishes relevant professional prospecting from consumer marketing and discusses generic legal-entity addresses. This is a country-specific starting point, not a blanket permission for an Italy-based sender. Confirm applicable sender/recipient requirements, the organisation/role and objections before launch; seek qualified advice if uncertain.
- Other European countries: no campaign has been cleared. Do not extrapolate the UK corporate-subscriber approach across Europe.
- Obtain the still-unidentified third opt-out and screen exact inbox, school, domain and previous-contact records. Keep suppression data private. Never replace the local suppression file with the public prospect research.
- Before sending, Zac must approve the actual primary recipients, final wording and timing. Recheck sending identity, reply forwarding and copied recipients. Deploy a new approved campaign tag first; no old scheduler should be enabled.
