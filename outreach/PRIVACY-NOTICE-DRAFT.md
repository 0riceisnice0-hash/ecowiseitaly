## Why we may contact schools and education professionals

We research and contact schools and relevant education professionals to introduce EcoWise Italy's tailored outdoor-education programmes, respond to enquiries, prepare proposals and manage professional relationships.

Where applicable, we rely on our legitimate interests in developing relevant school partnerships and promoting our services. Before using this basis, we assess whether the contact is relevant and balance our interests against the individual's rights and reasonable expectations. We do not use this basis where those interests are overridden, and we honour direct-marketing objections immediately.

## Information we use

We may use:

- a school or organisation name, website and postal address;
- a public role or departmental email address;
- a professional name, job title and work contact details where necessary;
- the public source and date on which a detail was checked;
- correspondence, enquiry details and communication history; and
- opt-out, objection, invalid-address and suppression records.

We do not buy consumer lists for this campaign, use sensitive personal information for targeting, or use automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects.

## Where the information comes from

Information may come directly from you, your school or organisation, public school websites and directories, and official public sources such as the UK government's Get Information about Schools service. If we contact you using information obtained elsewhere, this notice is made available at the first communication.

## Who receives it

Access is limited to EcoWise Italy and service providers used to host the website, provide email, maintain the outreach register or support business communications. Those providers may process information only for the agreed service and subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

## How long we keep it

Research records for prospects that do not engage are reviewed and normally deleted or anonymised within 12 months of the last verification or communication. Enquiry and customer records may be kept longer where needed for planning, contracts, accounting, safeguarding, legal claims or other legal obligations.

If you object or opt out, we retain the minimum information needed in a suppression record so that we do not contact you again. Suppression records are kept for as long as necessary to respect that request.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access to your personal information, correction, erasure or restriction, or object to its use. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing. Email `adamecorose@gmail.com` with “privacy” or “no thanks”; we will stop marketing and record the suppression.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Italy's data-protection authority: https://www.garanteprivacy.it/web/garante-privacy-en

## International services

Some technology providers may process information outside Italy or the European Economic Area. Before publication, EcoWise Italy will identify the chosen providers and confirm the transfer mechanism and safeguards used where required.

## Changes

We may update this notice when our practices or providers change. The current version and its update date will be published on this website.
