# School outreach legitimate-interests assessment — draft

> Owner/legal review required before approval. This assessment supports a narrow, manually reviewed pilot; it does not approve bulk sending or override the rules of a recipient's country.

Assessment date: 3 September 2026  
Campaign: EWI-SCHOOLS-2026-09-03-A  
Controller: EcoWise Italy — exact legal name to be confirmed  
Decision owner: Adam Rose

## 1. Purpose test

EcoWise Italy wants to introduce relevant outdoor-education programmes to schools that appear likely to organise overseas residential, ecology, outdoor-learning or co-curricular trips. The business purpose is specific, real and compatible with the professional functions of the targeted organisations.

Expected benefits include giving suitable schools another educational-trip option, filling year-round programme capacity and developing direct professional partnerships. The campaign is not aimed at pupils, parents in their personal capacity, sole traders or consumers.

## 2. Necessity test

A short email to a manually selected school role inbox is a proportionate way to ask for referral to the correct educational-visits contact. The pilot does not require sensitive information, behavioural profiling, tracking pixels, purchased consumer data or large-scale automated sending.

Less intrusive measures are used first:

- target only schools with an apparent curricular, age or residential fit;
- prefer general or departmental role inboxes over named individuals;
- send a small pilot before considering another reviewed wave;
- use one introduction and no more than one approved follow-up;
- link to detailed information rather than placing unnecessary personal data in the email; and
- stop immediately when a school or contact objects.

## 3. Balancing test

The intended recipients act in a professional capacity and may reasonably receive relevant supplier enquiries at a published school role inbox. The message is closely connected to school trips and outdoor education, clearly identifies EcoWise Italy, uses a valid reply/opt-out route and makes no misleading availability or package claims.

Possible impacts are unwanted email, time spent redirecting the enquiry, or unexpected use of a public work address. These risks are reduced by manual relevance checks, low frequency, no open-tracking pixel, transparent source records, immediate suppression and deletion/anonymisation review of non-engaging prospect data after 12 months.

The assessment does not cover an address until the reviewer confirms:

- the school or organisation is an appropriate legal/corporate recipient for the applicable electronic-marketing rules;
- the inbox is still publicly displayed by the school or an authoritative directory;
- the offer is relevant to the school's pupil ages and activities;
- the address is absent from the suppression register;
- the recipient's country rules have been checked; and
- the privacy information is available at the first communication where required.

## 4. Safeguards and operating decision

The pilot may proceed only when all launch gates in the campaign workbook are complete. Every send, bounce, referral, reply and objection must be recorded. An objection changes the send decision to `Do not contact` and adds the address or domain to the suppression register before any further campaign run.

Do not use this assessment for:

- personal Gmail, Outlook or similar consumer addresses;
- named contacts gathered from social profiles without a separate assessment;
- schools whose legal/subscriber status is unclear;
- bought, traded or unverifiable lists;
- automated high-volume sending;
- retargeting, tracking pixels or behavioural profiling; or
- a new audience, purpose, country or materially different campaign.
