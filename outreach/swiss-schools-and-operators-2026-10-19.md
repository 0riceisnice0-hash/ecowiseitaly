## How to approach

Schools: ask for the Head of Science/Biology, DP/ESS coordinator or educational visits lead. Lead with practical field investigation and the public Beau Soleil example, then offer the teacher pack. Tailor curriculum wording after confirming the school's actual programme.

Operators: ask for the educational product, contracting or partnerships team. Offer EcoWise Italy as the local educational delivery partner. Send the partner brief when relevant. Discuss supplier terms directly with Adam; no commission or package protection has been promised.

Read only the cited pages for the stated signals. A successful HTTP read does not establish inbox deliverability. Unresolved rows remain research tasks and must not be put into a send queue.
