## LinkedIn post: practical science

What changes when pupils have time to investigate a real landscape?

They have to notice, choose a method, record what they find and think about what the evidence actually supports. Field science connects the practical and theoretical parts of learning in a way a worksheet alone cannot.

EcoWise Italy recently shared its tenth annual five-day environmental science and field sampling trip with Beau Soleil College. Students practised terrestrial and aquatic field methods and collected data for DP biology and environmental studies projects.
