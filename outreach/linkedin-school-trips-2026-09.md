# LinkedIn post — school trips

What could an overseas school residential look like when environmental science, ecology, storytelling, environmental art and teamwork all take place in the same landscape?

For more than 20 years, EcoWise Italy has designed and tailored outdoor education programmes in Piemonte. We work with schools throughout the year, from shorter visits to five-day residentials and multi-week immersions.

Pupils are immersed in field science and real-world ecosystems through hands-on practical investigation and theoretical learning. They solve practical challenges, build confidence as a group and reconnect with nature away from screens.

EcoWise Italy designs the programme, selects the location, and books suitable accommodation and food on the school's behalf. Schools arrange their main travel and receive separate invoices from the relevant providers.
