# UK school outreach plan

This is the prepared September 2026 acquisition phase. It does not authorise sending. A human must review the final recipient list, sender identity and copy before any external message leaves an EcoWise Italy-controlled mailbox.

## Goal and audience

The first goal is qualified conversations with UK independent and international schools for residential outdoor education in Piemonte, especially underused November–March capacity. Prioritise schools with senior/prep pupils, boarding or international outlook, environmental/science programmes, IB provision, an established trips programme, and budgets for overseas residential learning.

Relevant roles, in order, are Head of Outdoor Education or Educational Visits Coordinator; Director of Co-curricular/Enrichment; Deputy Head; Head of Geography, Science or Environmental Systems; Trips Coordinator; IB Coordinator; and Headteacher. Start with a school-level role inbox where possible. Named personal addresses require a documented UK GDPR lawful-basis assessment and privacy information.

The second track is partnership outreach to specialist school-travel operators that can package and invoice qualifying travel while EcoWise Italy remains the educational delivery partner. Keep school and operator pipelines separate.

## Recipient research and compliance

- Use the Department for Education's [Get Information about Schools public downloads](https://www.get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/) for bulk establishment data. Its acceptable-use policy explicitly directs bulk users to the daily public downloads rather than automated HTML scraping.
- Use the Independent Schools Council's [Find a School directory](https://www.isc.co.uk/schools/) to qualify independent-school fit and find official school contact routes.
- Do not buy, rent or scrape a list into Mailchimp. [Mailchimp prohibits third-party, purchased and scraped audiences](https://mailchimp.com/help/requirements-best-practices-audiences/), even where a separate legal basis for an individual B2B email might exist.
- The ICO says unsolicited B2B email to a corporate subscriber does not require PECR consent, but the sender must identify itself, provide a valid opt-out address, respect objections, and still comply with UK GDPR when personal data is used. Follow the current [ICO business-to-business marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/) and obtain professional advice if the legal status of a recipient or campaign is unclear.
- Maintain an owned suppression list and screen every batch against it. Never email sole traders or individual subscribers without consent or another valid PECR route.
- Do not use tracking pixels in the cold introduction. Link tracking and analytics require separate privacy and PECR review.

Mailchimp remains appropriate for people who expressly subscribe or ask for ongoing information. The initial researched outreach should be small-batch, one-to-one-style email from an authenticated EcoWise Italy domain mailbox, with no invented personalisation and no attachment on the first touch.

## Launch sequence

1. **23 August–4 September:** verify the sender domain, SPF, DKIM and DMARC; publish/verify the privacy notice; build and manually qualify the first 50-school list; create the suppression register; test deliverability internally.
2. **Tuesday 8 September, 09:30 UK time:** send a manually approved pilot of 15–20 messages to role or general school inboxes. Watch bounces, complaints and replies for 48 hours.
3. **Thursday 10 September:** review the pilot. Stop if recipient quality, legal basis or sender reputation is weak; otherwise release the remaining reviewed first batch.
4. **Seven working days later:** one concise follow-up with a useful planning detail and a direct question. Do not send repeated follow-ups to non-responsive schools beyond the approved sequence.
5. **Late September:** a final value-led follow-up only to the best-fit unresponsive schools, then suppress them from this campaign.

Adam owns every response and promises a personal reply within 24 hours. Record school, source, role, message version, send date, response, next action, proposal and lost reason in an owner-controlled lead register.
